10 of 12 subject lines turn up in more than one problem, so merging on subject alone could not keep them apart.

parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so a date with a day of 12 or less swaps day and month: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July”. This covers reports from every tenant that came in by app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form.

hint: the booking-v3 form asks for the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and then reads the date in the tenant’s order, day first. People type what the hint says, and the date flips. The report reads like the parser bug, but the bug is in the form. It only comes through the booking form of tenants on booking-v3.

one-day: dates move one day earlier

These dates do not swap. They move one day back: “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” One report has the same meeting on Tuesday in Lisbon and on Monday in Toronto. That points at a booking date sent as a timestamp and shown in the recipient’s time zone. The desk already listed these reports as one-day moves, but counted them under parser.

After the engineering update

Engineering closed #4471 on 5 October.

  • parser: fixed by fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Check it before you close the ticket: harbour.dental writes en-GB, which writes the day first but is not an EU locale (see its tenant locales above). If the fix keeps a list of EU locales, those reports stay broken. This kb asks the platform whether a locale writes the day first, never a typed list; the fix should do the same.
  • hint: won’t fix, because booking-v3 is retired. In this export, alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour still run booking-v3. Confirm they have moved before you close the ticket.
  • one-day: no fix. Neither change touches it. New one-day reports go to its ticket, not to #4471.
